# Code of conduct

ConStack is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and professional community. Be constructive, assume good intent, and focus discussion on the work rather than the person.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and sharing another person's private information are not tolerated. Project maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository owner listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.
